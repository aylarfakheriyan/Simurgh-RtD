Context

Cultural narrative as material.

The Simurgh has existed for centuries across Persian literature, miniature painting, architecture, and collective imagination.

Rather than digitally reconstructing or simply representing a mythical creature, this project explores how immersive interaction can create new possibilities for engaging with cultural narratives.

Using the Simurgh as a case study, the project applies Research through Design (RtD) and Practice-Based Research (PBR) approaches to investigate how culturally informed VR experiences can influence interpretation, engagement, and embodied interaction.

Method

Research through Design.

The project combines:

Research through Design (RtD) · Practice-Based Research (PBR) · Cultural Narrative Analysis · Character and Environment Design · Interactive VR Prototyping · Qualitative Experience Evaluation

Contribution

Designing immersive cultural experiences.

This project explores an RtD approach for designing immersive cultural experiences by integrating cultural analysis, narrative interpretation, character design, interactive system development, and user experience evaluation.

The research investigates how Virtual Reality can move beyond passive visualization of heritage and support embodied encounters with symbolic meanings, cultural narratives, and collective imagination.

Prototype

Features.

Interactive Virtual Reality Environment · Narrative-Driven Exploration · Environmental Storytelling · Character-Based Cultural Interaction · Spatial Interaction · Immersive Audiovisual Experience

Evaluation

Learning through use.

The prototype was explored through qualitative user sessions focusing on user engagement, sense of immersion, interaction experience, cultural interpretation, and overall experience.

Insights from evaluation informed iterative refinement of the research prototype.

Perspective

Beyond visualization.

Rather than asking:

How can Virtual Reality visualize cultural heritage?

This project explores:

How can immersive experiences participate in the interpretation, engagement, and continued evolution of cultural knowledge?
